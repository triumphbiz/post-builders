Before you download, check

  • The image is a general scene. It is not presented as Dan, a real job or a real customer, and shows no fake certificate.
  • Never say a certificate is legally required for owner-occupiers. Say agents, buyers and insurers often ask for one.
  • Insurers "may" ask for evidence. Never suggest a claim will be refused.
  • Honest urgency only ("bookings are filling up"). No "only 3 spots left".
  • No phone number. The call to action is a free quote at smokealarmsnow.au.
  • Don't promise the certificate on the day, and don't say other electricians charge extra.
  • Australian spelling and no long dashes. Every word fits comfortably.
  • No invented reviews or testimonials.
